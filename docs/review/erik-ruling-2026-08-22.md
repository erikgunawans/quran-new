# Erik's ruling — the project stops waiting on the ustadz

> **Decided by Erik, 2026-08-22.** Recorded because a ruling of this weight must be a record, not
> four cross-references to a chat transcript. Same form as `rights-2026-08-20.md` and
> `rights-2026-08-21.md`, which are also Erik's own rulings written up.
> **This is Erik's decision. It is NOT a scholarly artefact and must never be cited as one.**

## What he decided

His words, verbatim: **"no need for ustadz letter anymore."**

Asked directly whether that covered only ISC-565's deploy or also ISC-417 and ISC-464(b), he
confirmed the **wide** reading: **"yes."**

## What it changes

**The waiting schedule, and nothing else.** The project no longer holds work open pending a reply
from Ustadz Ahmad Isrofiel Mardlatillah.

- **ISC-417 is WITHDRAWN and stays `[ ]` permanently.** Withdrawing a criterion does not satisfy it.
  It must never be re-marked `[x]`.
- **ISC-565 shipped without waiting** — deployed 2026-08-22, Worker version `2b7707f2`.
- The app ships **AI-authored religious answers with no scholarly sign-off**, indefinitely, by
  Erik's knowing decision.

## What it does NOT change

**The provenance.** Two letters — `tanya-ai-request-2026-08-17.md` and
`ustadz-followup-2026-08-18.md` — remain SENT and UNANSWERED. He has sent nothing in reply to
either. The resulting claim is **weaker** than before, never stronger: we are not less reviewed than
we thought, we are permanently unreviewed and now say so.

**Every promise inside those letters.** A promise delivered to a third party cannot be retired by
our own bookkeeping. Because we no longer wait, clauses of the form *"selama menunggu"* /
*"sebelum surat ini dijawab"* lose the closing event that was ours to bring about — so those
promises become **permanent**, not void. The binding sets are each letter's
§*Yang tidak kami lakukan* (four bullets and seven respectively) plus every waiting clause in their
bodies. **Anything not explicitly released stays held.** Releasing one requires an explicit,
item-by-item ruling from Erik.

**His three existing permissions**, which stand, do not widen, and cover none of this:
F-1 = yes (2026-07-17), co-display (2026-07-23), and the machine hadith Indonesian as-is
(2026-08-12 — VERBAL and relayed; the hadith TEXT layer only, not chapter titles, and on our own
narrow reading the Hadits page only, not the answer card — see that file's 2026-08-21 caveat).

**A future reply is still recordable.** If he answers, it is recorded per §*Setelah Ustadz menjawab*
— date, points approved or refused, and its form. Ending the wait does not make a real answer
unrecordable, and a note saying otherwise would itself breach a promise in the letters.

## Open, and Erik's alone to close

1. **ISC-464(b)** — the God/unseen filter. Held on the promise, not the schedule.
2. **The pinned hukum-nikah / hukum-waris lists.** Held.
3. **Hadith ordering** unchanged on our own technical grounds, and **`MAX_DISPLAY = 2`** unmoved.
   The cap is separately his own ruling (`rights-2026-08-20.md` §4).
4. **Two remedies that had no vehicle left.** One promise carried an exception for an already-shipped
   sentence, to be corrected "according to the Ustadz's answer"; another required a *next letter* to
   carry specific corrections. Both vehicles are gone, so both now hang open indefinitely. A
   temporary exception must not become permanent by default.

## Why the disclosures matter more now

`AI_CHIP` / `AI_NOTE` (`web/src/answer-disclosure.ts`), the hadith layer's *"Terjemahan mesin ·
belum ditinjau"*, and the kajian artefacts' *"belum diperiksa ulama"* are each pinned by tests and
are load-bearing in a way they were not before. They must not be softened, made conditional, or
removed. They are not the only safeguard — the answer guard is the other — but they are the part the
reader sees.
