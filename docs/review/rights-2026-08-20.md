## 1. The English narration is withdrawn from publication

**Asked:** the hadith card renders `h.english` — sunnah.com's narration — verbatim, with the
Darussalam / Muhsin Khan translator credit. `docs/review/hadith-id-approval-2026-08-12.md` records
sunnah.com's terms as **"private research use"**, and a public card is neither private nor research.
The question had been raised in six consecutive handoffs and never actually PUT.

**Ruled (2026-08-20):** stop publishing it. The translator credit goes with it.

**Scope, stated precisely because the first implementation got it wrong:** what was withdrawn is
PUBLICATION, not possession. `h.english` stays on the record and stays in the model's user message
(`web/src/answer-contract.ts`) — the model may reason over it and must never reproduce it.

**Incomplete for one commit.** `21ed3c9` removed it from the RENDERER, and the evidence recorded was
a grep of the served client bundle. That instrument could not see the actual leak: `/api/answer` and
`/api/dalil` were still serving `english` and `translator` in their JSON bodies — unpainted rather
than unpublished. Closed on 2026-08-20 (late) by `publishedCardOf`
(`worker/src/dalil.ts`), a field-by-field projection with an exact-SET test, applied on both routes.

## 2. The English chapter and kitab titles are withdrawn

**Asked (2026-08-20, late):** `bab_en` still rendered on answer cards and reference rows. The
2026-08-20 ruling above named the narration, and a comment in `web/src/hadith-card.ts` had recorded
`bab_en` as deliberately untouched — *"a heading is a different artifact from the narration"*.

**Ruled:** withdraw `bab_en`. On the follow-up, **withdraw `book_en` too** — the same sunnah.com
English editorial apparatus, one level further up. Neither renders anywhere now and neither rides
the wire.

**Cost:** the reference line no longer names the kitab. It still says which record it is, how it is
graded, and where to read it.

## 3. The machine Indonesian is KEPT

**Asked (2026-08-20, late):** `machine_id`, the Indonesian shown to readers, was MACHINE-TRANSLATED
FROM the English just withdrawn. Does the withdrawal reach it?

**Ruled:** keep it.

**What this ruling does NOT settle, stated because a comment briefly claimed otherwise.** The
derivative-work principle — whether publishing a machine translation of text you may not publish is
itself publication — **was not asked and is not decided.** It was given as context for the
keep/withdraw choice; Erik answered the choice. A comment in `web/src/hadith-card.ts` was written
asserting *"derivation is not publication of the thing derived from"* as though it were part of the
ruling. It was not, and was corrected the same session.

**What the ruling does rest on, unchanged:** Ustadz Ahmad's **VERBAL, relayed** approval covers
DISPLAYING this Indonesian (`docs/review/hadith-id-approval-2026-08-12.md` — *"Form: VERBAL,
relayed. Not written."*), and it keeps its `belum ditinjau` badge. `reviewed_id` stays pure:
permitted is not reviewed (ISC-448).

## 4. `MAX_DISPLAY = 2` is EDITORIAL, not a licensing limit

**Ruled (2026-08-20):** the two-card cap is a judgement about what an ANSWER should carry — the two
strongest receipts, not a reading list. The licensing justification could not survive being read
next to the browse page, which publishes the entire Ṣaḥīḥayn: two cards and a whole collection
cannot both be what sunnah.com About §8 permits.

**The number does not move.** It never rested on the licensing claim; what changed is only what the
comment claims for it.

**§8 is not thereby disposed of.** If it binds anything here it binds the BROWSE page, which is where
mass reproduction actually happens. Open item, recorded rather than dropped.

### The contradiction this created, and how it was resolved

`docs/review/ustadz-followup-2026-08-18.md` — **SENT 2026-08-19, UNANSWERED** — tells Ustadz Ahmad
the opposite in its own words:

> *"batas dua hadis itu bukan pilihan tata letak kami, dan bukan pula perkara keilmuan. Batas itu
> berasal dari syarat perizinan sunnah.com … jawaban Ustadz tidak dapat menggeser batas tersebut."*

The reclassification landed one day after that letter went out, and the letter's own Bagian 1 is an
apology for three earlier false descriptions of the app.

**Ruled (2026-08-20, late): reconcile the code; send the ustadz nothing.** The cap he was promised is
the cap he gets, and the basis was never something he was asked to rule on. Four contradicting
comments across `worker/src/dalil.ts`, `web/src/hadith-card.ts` and `web/src/dalil-search.ts` were
reconciled to point at the one canonical statement (the `MAX_DISPLAY` docblock).

**A distinction the old comments conflated, kept separate from here on:** the COUNT is editorial;
what remains a genuine rights position is that everything the cap excludes carries **no hadith text
at all**, only a reference line. Those are separable.
