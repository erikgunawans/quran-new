## 5. sunnah.com About §8 does not bind the Hadits browse page

**Asked (2026-08-21).** §8 permits per-entry didactic use and forbids mass reproduction. `MAX_DISPLAY
= 2` was reclassified as EDITORIAL on 2026-08-20 precisely because the licensing justification could
not survive being read next to the browse page — two cards and a whole collection cannot both be what
§8 permits. That reclassification did not dispose of §8; it relocated it. If §8 binds anything here it
binds the browse page, which publishes the entire Ṣaḥīḥayn. It was put on 2026-08-21 and answered the
same minute.

*(A first draft of this paragraph said the question "had been recorded as open across fifteen
handoffs". That is false and nothing in the tree supports it. The §8-vs-BROWSE-PAGE framing was
created on **2026-08-20** by `6ede5cf`, so it was open for **one day**. The "fifteenth handoff"
figure belongs to the unanswered LETTERS, a different open item, and was carried across into this
one. A soft number inflating our own negligence is still a soft number, and it sits in the paragraph
that justifies a precision record — the same place the 2026-08-20 file already had to correct
"29 citations across ten files" down to 10 across 4.)*

**Ruled (2026-08-21): the browse page stays as it is.**

**The reasoning, which is Erik's ruling and not a legal opinion:** what §8 can protect is sunnah.com's
own work. The browse page does not carry that work.

- **The hadith TEXT is canonical public-domain Arabic.** The English translation in the source is
  licensed "private research use" and is **dropped at build time** — `src/app/build-hadith.ts`, stated
  in the header of `web/src/hadith.ts`. What a reader sees is the classical Arabic, which sunnah.com
  did not author and does not own.
- **The structural metadata is ours** — collection, kitab, bab, number, grade — and the Indonesian
  kitab titles are **authored in this repo**, not taken from sunnah.com's English apparatus
  (`web/src/hadith-titles.ts`, and see its own header on why a shelf label is not commentary).
- **The English apparatus is already withdrawn.** `book_en` and `bab_en` went on 2026-08-20 (ruling 2)
  and, as of the 2026-08-21 deploy, no longer ride the wire on either public route.
- **Attribution stays.** Every record links back to its source record on sunnah.com.

**The residual exposure, stated rather than argued away.** §8 is a **terms-of-use condition**, and a
condition can bind conduct even where copyright does not reach it — a site may condition access on
terms that ask more than copyright could compel. This ruling is that the reproduction §8 is aimed at
is the reproduction of sunnah.com's *work*, and that this page reproduces none of it. That is a
reading, and a reading can be wrong. It is recorded here so that if it is ever revisited, what is
revisited is an argument and not a silence.

**What would reopen this:** sunnah.com asking us to stop; a change that puts any sunnah.com-authored
English back on the browse page; or shipping the browse corpus in a form that is a redistribution of
their *dataset* rather than a rendering of the Arabic.

**Not changed by this ruling:** `MAX_DISPLAY = 2` stays EDITORIAL. It never rested on §8, and this
ruling does not hand it back a licensing basis. The genuine rights position beside it is unchanged —
everything the cap excludes carries **no hadith text at all**, only a reference line.

## 6. The machine Indonesian is left BROKEN on three surfaces, deliberately

**Found (2026-08-21), while verifying the deploy in ruling 5's session — not looked for.** The
machine-translated Indonesian (ISC-449) has **never rendered** on three of the four surfaces the
project believed it rendered on:

| surface | call site | renders? |
|---|---|---|
| Hadits browse page | `web/src/sections.ts:225` (passes the route slug) | **yes** |
| Hadits search results | `web/src/dalil-search.ts:127` | **no, never** |
| Fikih search results | same call site | **no, never** |
| Hadith card under an answer | `web/src/main.ts:545` | **no, never** |

**The mechanism.** Both broken call sites pass `h.collection` straight off the wire, which is a
DISPLAY NAME — `"Sahih Muslim"`. The shards are keyed by SLUG — `web/public/hadith-id/muslim/1.json`.
The fetch therefore asks for `/hadith-id/Sahih Muslim/1.json`, which does not exist; the SPA fallback
returns `index.html` at **HTTP 200**, `res.json()` throws on the HTML — and `loadHadithIds`
(`web/src/hadith-id.ts:74-81`) **catches that itself** and returns an EMPTY file. `hadithIdText` then
returns `null`, `if (id)` is false, and `machine_id` is simply never assigned. No error, no failing
test, no telemetry.

*(A first draft of this paragraph — and a source comment reading "THIS BRANCH IS NOT AN EDGE CASE —
IT IS EVERY CALL" — said the failure surfaced through the `catch` at the CALL SITES. It does not.
That `catch` fires on **zero** calls, because the inner one swallows the throw first. The OUTCOME
stated here was measured and is right; the ROUTE was read off the code and read wrong, in a record
whose own next paragraph boasts that the finding was measured rather than read. Whoever is one day
allowed to fix this would have gone looking for a hot `catch` that never runs.)*

**Two dates, not one.** The answer-card call site (`web/src/main.ts`) dates from **`e80ff9f`**,
2026-08-13, the commit that opened the hadith wall. The two SEARCH surfaces share
`web/src/dalil-search.ts:127` and date from **`734c577`**, **2026-08-18** — five days younger, and
2026-08-18 is the very date the follow-up letter singles out as the one it regrets most. A first
draft filed all three under `e80ff9f`, which is the flattering direction: it makes the search
surfaces look like inherited breakage rather than something added after the promise was made.

**Measured with a paired arm, using the SHIPPED function on LIVE prod cards** — not read off the
code. `hydrateMachineIndonesian` over the two cards prod's `/api/dalil` returned: **0/2** filled with
the wire value, **2/2** filled with the slug. A single arm here would have proved nothing, because a
probe that fills nothing is indistinguishable from a probe that is broken.

**Ruled (2026-08-21): DO NOT FIX IT.**

The reasoning is not that the bug is harmless. It is that repairing the key would **widen a standing
breach from one surface to four**, while **question 3 of the 2026-08-18 letter — which names these
exact three surfaces (`:254-257`) — is unanswered**. That is the same shape as the 2026-08-18 Fikih
addition, made one day after the promise and already apologised for in that letter's Bagian 1. Doing
it again knowingly is worse than doing it once by inattention.
