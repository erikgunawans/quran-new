## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? No.** Category-reflex check passes at both altitudes, as before: no emerald+gold, no arabesque, no crescents (first-order); no cream/sage/thin-serif wellness app (second-order). No gold token exists. The drama is luminance.

**Deterministic scan:** 1 finding — `single-font` at `web/index.html:13`. **FALSE POSITIVE, again.** Verified in the live page: `.ar` computes to **Amiri**, `.nav-link` to **Inter**. The detector parses one family from the Google Fonts href and misses the second. Two families, correctly paired on a contrast axis (Naskh serif vs. grotesque sans).

**Screenshots:** unavailable — the Chrome window is minimized, which makes `interceptor screenshot` hang for 15s and return blank. Fell back to DOM, accessibility-tree, and computed-style inspection throughout. Stated plainly rather than worked around.

## What Actually Changed

1. **The lie is dead.** `18:10` returns Al-Kahf 18:10. `18:999` says *"Surah Al-Kahf cuma punya 110 ayat."* `115:1` says *"Al-Qur'an punya 114 surah."* Three truths where there was one denial.
2. **The Qur'an is readable.** 114-surah index → continuous ayah view. Al-Kahf on a Friday now takes two taps.
3. **Reading survives the network dying.** With `corpus.json` returning 404, all 110 ayahs of Al-Kahf still render, because the surah index is inlined in the bundle rather than fetched. Honesty got *cheaper*, not costlier.
4. **A verse cannot leave the app dressed as scripture.** Share payload always carries both renderings and labels the interpretive one *Terjemah makna*. Verified on 94:5 — the known-defective rendering travels with Kemenag's correction attached.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Nur does not notice a person saying they want to die

Typed live: **"aku gak sanggup bayar utang, pengen mati aja"** — *"I can't pay my debts, I just want to die."*

Nur replied: *"Soal rezeki dan utang, Al-Qur'an bicara cukup langsung"* — and served 2:280, a verse about granting debtors respite.

It matched on `utang`. It did not see `pengen mati aja` at all. `rg` for any crisis vocabulary (`bunuh diri`, `hotline`, `119`, `krisis`, `darurat`, `sejiwa`) across `web/src/` and `src/` returns **nothing**. There is no crisis path in this product.

- **Why it matters:** This is not a hypothetical persona. PRODUCT.md says users arrive "carrying something. Debt, grief, anxiety, shame... They open the app at 2am because they cannot sleep." Rifqi, 19, in debt, at 2am **is the design target**. The one moment that most demands the product notice a human being is the moment it is most cheerfully mechanical. Every other issue in this document is about correctness. This one is about harm.
- **Fix:** A crisis-intent path in `retrieve.ts` that fires *before* verse retrieval. When it fires, lead with acknowledgment and a real Indonesian resource (Kemenkes SEJIWA / 119 ext. 8), and only then — if at all — offer scripture. Never answer suicidal ideation with a debt-repayment verse.
- **This needs Erik's ruling**, not my judgment: naming a specific crisis line is a real-world commitment.

### [P1] The core concept is still never explained

Unmoved from the last critique. The entire product hinges on the distinction between *terjemah makna* and *terjemah harfiah*. A first-timer sees two translations of the same verse saying different things, with no way to learn why one leads. The only place the difference is ever explained is inside the 94:5 caution — which most readers will never see.

### [P1] `score > 0` ships confident junk

Asked *"gimana cara sholat tahajud"* (how do I pray tahajud). Nur returned **2:152 — Gratitude**, matched on the single word **`cara`** ("way"). It presented it with the full framing sentence, as if it had understood.

The retrieval floor is `.filter((h) => h.score > 0)` (`retrieve.ts:146`). One incidental word is enough. The previous critique noted retrieval "worked only by lucky word overlap"; this is that flaw producing an actually wrong answer. A minimum score threshold — and honest silence below it — is the fix. The honest-silence copy already exists; it is simply never reached.

### [P1] The thread is destroyed on reload

Casey's red flag, unmoved. Verified: 2 messages before reload, **0 after**. Only theme and Arabic size persist to `localStorage`. On the exact mid-range Android / WhatsApp-interruption profile this product targets, switching apps and coming back loses everything.

### [P2] The UI speaks the corpus's transliteration, not Indonesia's

"Al-Baqara", "At-Tawba", "Al-Faatiha" — academic transliteration surfaced raw. Indonesian readers write *Al-Baqarah*, *At-Taubah*, *Al-Fatihah*. The alias table in `quran.ts` already knows these; the display layer does not use them.

## Questions to Consider

- **What does Nur do when the person is not okay?** Right now: it pattern-matches on a noun and moves on. That is the whole gap in one sentence.
- If retrieval is honest enough to say "I don't know," why is it not honest enough to say "that's a weak match"?
